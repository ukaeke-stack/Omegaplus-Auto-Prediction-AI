Platform features

Tools for the full prediction workflow

From fixture research to reviewing settled results, the app brings its main analysis and account tools into one workspace. Availability can depend on account access, supported data and configured providers.

⚽

Football Fixture Analyzer

Explore supported fixtures, leagues, dates and markets with AI-assisted analysis and available statistical context.

🏀

Basketball & Other Sports

Access basketball analysis and separate tools for additional supported sports, where data providers are available.

✦

AI Text & Voice Assistant

Describe a football selection request in natural language. Use text or supported browser voice input to help structure a request.

▦

Market & Slip Builder

Review market options, select outcomes, manage a slip and request supported booking-code generation when permitted.

▧

Booking-Code Image

Save a reviewed selection image and include the generated booking code after successful code generation.

◷

Daily Best & History

Review quality-qualified daily selections and use prediction history to inspect recorded outcomes over time.

◎

Correct Score & Statistics

Use supported score analysis, match statistics, results and prediction-performance information.

⇄

Result Verification

Review settled predictions and recorded results. Data coverage depends on the connected sports-data sources.

♧

Accounts & Membership

Sign in to your account and access features based on the current membership and administrator settings.

♧

Support & Admin Tools

Send feedback and support messages. Authorized administrators can manage users and app announcements.

Device Cache & Sync

♢

Notifications

In-app notification features can surface relevant updates. Delivery of background push notifications depends on device and service configuration.
